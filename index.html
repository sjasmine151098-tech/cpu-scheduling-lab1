CPU Scheduling Virtual Lab

Choose a scheduling model, enter your processes, and get the Gantt chart, CT, TAT and WT. Then compare its average WT and TAT with the other models.

1. Choose your model

Select the scheduling algorithm you want to run.

2. Enter process details

Arrival time (AT) can be 0 or more. Burst time (BT) must be 1 or more. Priority is used only by the Priority model.

ProcessArrival time (AT)Burst time (BT)PriorityRemove

Formulas used

CT = time when the process finishes TAT = CT − AT WT = TAT − BT

Ties are broken by earlier arrival time, then by process number. If no process is ready, the CPU stays idle until the next arrival.

4. Compare with other models

Tick the models you want to compare against your selected model. The comparison uses the same process data.

Numbers under each Gantt chart are time stamps. The number at the right end of a process block is that process's completion time (CT).
